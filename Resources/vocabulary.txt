# AudioRecord vocabulary — domain terms the speech models must spell correctly.
#
# Format: one entry per line; "#" starts a comment; "term: alias, alias" lists other
# spellings of the same term. Duplicates are merged. After editing, rebuild and install:
#     scripts/build-app.sh --install
#
# How each kind of entry is used:
#   single word   (sertraline)          corrected when the Parakeet engine mishears it
#                                        (acoustic match of the audio against the term)
#   phrase        (tardive dyskinesia)  hint for the Apple engine only — put rare words of
#                                        a phrase on their own line to get corrections
#   ACRONYM       (PHQ-9)               hint for the Apple engine; also fixes spelling of an
#                                        exact hearing (PHQ9 → PHQ-9)
# Only list words the models get wrong. Ordinary words (tapering, program) are already
# transcribed correctly and only cause wrong replacements of similar-sounding words.
